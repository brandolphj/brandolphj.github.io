About Me

> PERSONNEL FILE: JARED BRANDOLPH

Background

I graduated from the University of Pittsburgh with a degree in Information Science and Cybercrime.

My professional background includes IT support and corporate security, where I've worked with technical systems, security processes, and the people who depend on them.

What interests me most about cybersecurity is figuring out how things work, understanding where they can go wrong, and finding practical ways to make them more secure.

What I'm Working On

I'm continuing to develop my technical skills through hands-on labs and projects involving cloud security, identity and access management, security operations, and governance, risk, and compliance.

I use this website to document what I'm learning, explain my investigations, and share the results of projects I'm working on.

Beyond the Terminal

Cybersecurity isn't my only interest. I enjoy exploring technology, learning new things, and occasionally going down rabbit holes that have absolutely nothing to do with security.

That's part of why I created this website. It's a place for my professional projects, personal interests, interesting discoveries, and whatever side quests come along.
